# Disc Arena

A first-person action game in a glowing digital world: throw a light disc, fight with a staff, shield and gauntlets,
and run, slide and wall-jump through a rogue AI's system with up to 4 friends in co-op, or duel in the PvP colosseum.

## Download and play (Windows 64-bit)

1. Download **DiscArena_2026-11-12.zip** from the [latest release](https://github.com/Wes-inblack/DiscArena/releases/latest) (about 360 MB).
2. Unzip it and double-click `DiscArena\DiscArena.exe`.
3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the game isn't code-signed).

Hosting, joining, online play, controls and known issues are in [README.txt](README.txt) (also inside the zip).
If the game reports a missing DLL, run `DiscArena\Engine\Extras\Redist\en-us\vc_redist.x64.exe` from the zip.

Made with Unreal Engine 5.7.
